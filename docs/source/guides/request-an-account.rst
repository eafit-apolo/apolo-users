Request an account
==================

:Authors: Byron Arenilla
:Maintainer: Byron Arenilla
:Last reviewed: 2026-09-28
:Applies to: New users

To compute on Apolo you need two accounts, both created by the Apolo staff: a
**VPN account**, to reach the clusters, and a **cluster account**, to log in
and run jobs. This guide explains who can get them and how to ask for them.

Who can use Apolo
-----------------

- **Researchers of Universidad EAFIT** compute for free.
- **Other universities, academic institutions and companies** can also
  compute on Apolo. Their use is billed in **core-hours** (one hour of one
  processor core at 100 % use), **GPU-hours** and **TiB-months** of storage.
  To get a quote, write to apolo@eafit.edu.co.

Apolo is a good fit for your work if your software:

#. is scientific software,
#. runs on Linux,
#. is parallel or can be parallelized, and ideally
#. can run distributed across several nodes.

Ask for the accounts
--------------------

#. Send an email to apolo@eafit.edu.co with this information:

   - Username you would like
   - Email address
   - First name and last name
   - Cell phone number
   - Research or working group
   - Type of user: undergraduate, master's, PhD or researcher
   - Main resource you will use: CPU, memory, GPU or disk
   - Operating system of the computer you will connect from
   - Applications you plan to use on Apolo
   - Times when you are available for an introductory meeting

#. The Apolo staff schedules a meeting with you, in person or online, to
   check:

   - whether your software is already installed or needs to be installed,
   - whether it is configured to use the cluster efficiently, and
   - whether you need guidance to use Apolo.

#. After the meeting, the staff creates your VPN account and your cluster
   account and sends you the credentials.

.. important::

   Your accounts are personal and non-transferable. Do not share your
   credentials, even with members of your own group: each person must
   request their own account.

Files
-----

The next section is "Files"; when you click it, the "Home Directory" option appears.

.. image:: images/figura4.png
   :alt: Files drop-down menu
   :width: 400px

Once you open 'Home Directory' you will see all your files and directories; this is your home folder, the same one that opens when you access via terminal. This is your space, here you can create anything you might need.
